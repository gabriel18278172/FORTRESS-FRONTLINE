How to play
Drag a tower from the panel onto any open spot that is not on the track, like a classic drag-and-place tower defense game. Once placed, a tower stays exactly where you put it. Tap a tower to upgrade, change targeting, or sell it. Waves start automatically. Every 10th wave brings a powerful elite enemy. Waves continue forever and gradually get harder. Credits are awarded after each wave. Elite enemies have special defenses.
